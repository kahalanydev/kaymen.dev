The segmented course, in the font it was meant to be in

Same arch you picked. Two things changed: the wordmark actually renders in Sora now, and the arch has three optical cuts so it stops turning into a solid band at small sizes.

The serif was a bug, not a choice. SVG text does not always repaint when a webfont arrives after the markup is injected, and the SVG default fallback is serif rather than sans. Now styled by class with a real fallback chain, and nothing draws until the fonts are ready.

Three optical cuts

You flagged nothing here, but I did: nine visible gaps is a lot of detail, and below about 120px they close up. Rather than accept that, the arch is redrawn with fewer and wider segments as it shrinks, the way a type family cuts display and text separately.

The variants that have to exist

A gradient-free, one-colour form for print, embroidery, stamps and anywhere a background is not white. Drawn now, because these are the ones that get improvised badly later.

In place

The only test that counts. A logo looks fine on a slide and falls apart in a 16px tab.

Where this stands

This is ready to be real. What exists now is a display cut, a text cut, a mark, the deep and one-colour variants, and a favicon that is legible at 16px. That is a working set, not a picture of one.

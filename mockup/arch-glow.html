The course taking the light

The grey stones light in order from the keystone down both legs, then it starts again. The keystone itself never moves — it is the one stone carrying weight, and washing light over it flattens the thing the mark exists to point at. All four are the same wave; only the tempo and the peak differ. Each is drawn as a background image at 169px, which is exactly what the rail does.

# Client Agreement

**Between** Kaymen Group LLC ("we", "us") **and** [CLIENT LEGAL NAME] ("you")

**Drafted 19 August 2026. NOT REVIEWED BY A LAWYER.**

> **What this document is for.** kaymen.dev publishes four commitments on its
> home page — you own the code, you own the data, you own the servers, and you
> can take the whole thing with you at any time. Those are the reason a client
> chooses us over a subscription. **Published on a web page they are marketing;
> written here they are enforceable**, and a client who is being asked to leave

4.3 Tell us promptly if you think an account has been compromised.

## 5. Ownership, and the right to leave

**This clause is the reason this agreement exists. It is not negotiable
downwards by us.**

5.1 **The code is yours.** On payment of the build fee, all intellectual property
in the bespoke software we build for you transfers to you absolutely. We keep no
licence back over it beyond what clause 5.5 reserves.

5.2 **The data is yours.** It always was. We claim no ownership, no licence to
use it, and no right to mine, aggregate, resell or train on it.

5.3 **The infrastructure is yours.** Servers and services are in your accounts,
or transfer to your accounts on request at no charge.

5.4 **You may leave at any time.** On request, at any point and for any reason,
we will hand over:
   - the complete source code, with its version history;
   - a complete export of the database in a documented, non-proprietary format;
   - all files, assets and credentials;
   - the deployment configuration and written instructions to run it elsewhere.

   We will do this **within [14] days**, **at no charge**, and **whether or not
   you owe us money** — a disputed invoice is a debt to be collected, not a
   reason to hold a business hostage. We will not degrade, time-bomb, obfuscate
   or withhold any part of a system to encourage you to stay.

5.5 **What we keep.** Generic know-how, techniques, and any pre-existing or
general-purpose components we owned before this engagement or built for use
across clients. Where such a component is embedded in your system we grant you a
perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use, modify and sublicense it as
part of that system. You will never need our permission to keep running what we
built for you.

5.6 **Portfolio.** We would like to describe this work publicly. We will not name
you, show your interface, or link to your site without your written agreement,
and you may withdraw that agreement later. Any screenshot we publish is taken
from a local installation containing invented data — never from your live system
and never from a copy of it.

6.1 Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and

6.2 It does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the

## 7. Warranties and liability

7.1 We warrant that we will perform with reasonable skill and care, and that the
software we build does not knowingly infringe anybody else's intellectual
property.

7.2 **We do not warrant that software is free of defects.** No one honestly can.
What we commit to is fixing them: for **[90] days** after go-live we will correct
faults in the delivered work at no charge, and after that the monthly fee covers
it for as long as you are on it.

7.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of
profit, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings.

7.4 Our total liability under this agreement is limited to **[the fees you paid
us in the twelve months before the claim]**.

7.5 Nothing in this agreement limits liability for death or personal injury
caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be
limited.

## 8. Term and termination

8.1 The build runs until the Schedule 1 milestones are met. The monthly service
then runs month to month.

8.2 **You may cancel the monthly service on [30] days' written notice.** There is
no minimum term, no cancellation fee, and no notice period longer than a month.
[UNLESS a partnership arrangement under clause 8.5 applies.]

8.3 We may terminate on [60] days' written notice — deliberately longer than
yours, because you need time to arrange a replacement and we do not.

8.4 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied
within [30] days of written notice.

8.5 **[OPTIONAL — partnership arrangement.]** Where the build fee is waived or
reduced in exchange for a committed monthly term, state the term and what
happens on early exit here. Without this clause, clause 8.2 governs.

8.6 On termination for any reason, clause 5.4 applies. There is no exit fee.
